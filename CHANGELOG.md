# Changelog

Twin Embers is a port of Pokémon FireRed (pret/pokefirered) to the Nintendo 3DS. This file lists what changed for the player in each release. It does not repeat the detail of the [documentation](docs/).

## v0.7.0

### Added

- **Online and local wireless play (test)**: the Union Room, the Wireless Club's trades and battles work between two consoles, over the internet or nearby. The game's own wireless library talks to an emulated Wireless Adapter; *Online* goes through a relay (a small server that only passes sealed frames on), *Local* uses the 3DS's own radio. OPTIONS' Settings page has *Multiplayer* (Online or Local) and *Online room* (the relay's public room, or a private room of a 16-digit code, made at random by the NEW key of the keypad and typed by your friends, for playing with them only). A build can carry a relay of its own (`TWIN_RELAY=host:port`); `sdmc:/3ds/twinembers/online.cfg` picks another one or a private code. The releases are built with the author's public relay, `82.70.228.148:47311` (TCP). A full public room sends the console on to the next. Both consoles keep 60 fps while linked; over the internet the link rooms (walking to the trade PC) are as slow as the round trip between the consoles. The internet frames are sealed with ChaCha20-Poly1305 under a key made from the room code (a private room's takes 10000 rounds of PBKDF2 and its name is made from another half of the output, so a six-digit PIN is no longer the only thing between a stranger and the room; a six-digit code of an earlier test build is dropped, and OPTIONS says so; the 64 bytes of key and name take two PBKDF2 blocks, about 2 times the work of the public room's key and 4.9 times for a private one, not yet timed on a console); the code is our own, checked against the RFC test vectors but not reviewed by a cryptographer. How to run a relay: [docs/RELAY-SERVER.md](docs/RELAY-SERVER.md). Checked on a computer and in two Azahar emulators over the internet; the local radio and real consoles are not tested yet.
- **Pokemon to surf on** (Emerald Lens 1.1.1): *More characters to be* also downloads eleven blobs for the player surfing (Lapras, Blastoise, Dewgong, Latias, Ludicolo, Mantine, Pelipper, Pikachu, Tentacruel, Wailmer, Whiscash), and a fourth row on OPTIONS' looks page, *Surf Pokemon*, picks one with a preview of it and its artist (default: FireRed's own). Emerald Lens' head overlay is not used. Checked on a computer only.
- **Five more player looks from Emerald Lens 1.2.0** for the Builder's *More characters to be*, so there are 26 in all: Hilbert, Hilda, Nate and Rosa in HGSS style (by DiegoWT) and a Red RGBY look (by hyo, from Poffin_Case's FireRed/LeafGreen-style overworlds). They are overworld looks: in battle and on the Trainer Card the game's own picture stays. The sheets are pinned at Emerald Lens' commit 67a25e2 by size and SHA-256, like the others; nothing already pinned changed. Emerald Lens 1.2.0 also has two LIBERTY looks (by tebited15, from LibertyTwins) that are **not** included.
- Three more player looks from Emerald Lens 1.1.1 for the Builder's *More characters to be*: Harmony (D/P), Peach (HGSS) and Aang, so there are 21 in all. The sheets are pinned at Emerald Lens' commit 9a97879 by size and SHA-256; a look without a back, front or intro picture keeps FireRed's own.
- **More Nuzlocke and Randomlocke options** (OPTIONS, Nuzlocke section, now two pages). Nuzlocke gets *Set mode*: the battle style is Set while the rules are on, so there is no free switch after a foe faints. Randomlocke moves to its own page and gets *Random for* (both, wild only or trainers only; the starter and gifts follow the wild side) and *Legendaries* (allow them, or never pick a legendary or mythical species). All are off or at their old behaviour by default.

- **Wireless screens on the lower screen (test)**: in the Union Room the lists (what to do with a player, which group to join, the board), the leader's wait (START and CANCEL, with how many players are missing), the trade's choice of a Pokémon (both parties as cards, one tab each) and the chat's keyboard (ABC, abc, symbols and saved phrases, Delete, Send, Quit) are drawn on the lower screen with buttons and chosen with a tap; the game's own windows stay on the upper screen. Checked on a computer only; not yet played on the emulator or a console.

- **Poke Ball style, now the default** (test): OPTIONS > Graphics has a *Style* row, Poke Ball (a plain red, white pills for what is chosen, dark-red pills for the rest, white notes with a thin dark-red edge, exit keys in white with a dark outline, no embers; the default) or Embers (the red look with sparks, as before). It changes every view at once. A settings.cfg without a *style* line is Poke Ball; the choice of Embers is kept as `style=0`. The Bag, the shops, the choices, OPTIONS (its look rows with the look's preview, and the keypad of the private room's code), SAVE, the Trainer Card and the Pokédex (menu, lists, habitats, a Pokémon's page) take the whole lower screen with a dark band between a red upper half and a white note; HOME and the other menus keep their layout in the new colours.

- **Cheats page** (OPTIONS, a fifth section with its own gold page dot; all off until chosen, kept in `settings.cfg`, not in the save): *Sure catch* (every Poke Ball catches), *Shiny odds* (Normal, 1/4096, 1/1024, 1/256, 1/64, 1/16 or Always; a Pokémon you will own is made shiny with that chance, keeping its gender and nature, Unown excepted, and the game's own odds stay), *No encounters* (no random wild encounters in grass, caves, water or Rock Smash; fishing and scripted ones still work), *Through walls* (you pass walls and objects, never the map's edge), *EXP rate* (x1, x2, x4 or x8 for a won battle), *Infinite PP* (your moves never lose PP) and *Always flee* (running away from a wild battle always works). The page's seven rows, the shiny search and the game hooks (recipe 0278) are checked on a computer; the hooks are not built or played yet.

### Changed

- **Upper-screen battle boxes and message box redrawn**: the Pokemon health boxes (yours and the foe's) are white with a black outline, a red cap, red level mark and a red line under the name; the HP bar is a black capsule with ticks at half and a fifth and a striped fill when low, so it reads without its colour; your EXP strip is black with no red in it. The message box is white with black text, a red fillet inside a black line and a red prompt arrow. Checked on a computer (the box code is rendered with the game's own font); not yet on a console.
- **Move to forget and move relearner on the whole lower screen** (Poke Ball style): learning a move with four known and REMEMBER (the Two Island man) now have the Poke Ball look of the Bag and the Pokédex: the question across the red top, the Pokémon on a white card (its picture, level, nickname and the chosen move's PP, power and accuracy), five pills for the moves with the type's badge at their end (the new move and CANCEL dark), the move's description on the white and BACK on the band. The relearner's two scroll arrows are at the top right, and its questions and messages are a white card over the dimmed list with YES and NO as pills. The Poke Ball band no longer has a round button in the middle of it. Checked on a computer only; the Ascuas style is as it was.
- **Naming keyboard and Easy Chat in the Poke Ball look**: the naming keyboard (you, a Pokémon, a box) has the boxes of the name across the top of the red ground, the three tabs with DELETE and OK, the keys as pills (the cursor's white with a red letter) and, under the dark band, the question and its hint with the Pokémon's picture. Easy Chat (the phrase, the group and letter menus, a group's words with their rail and letter strip, and the Yes/No questions) keeps its layout but uses the same pieces: white cards, dark-red pills, a white pill for what is chosen, a pink one for the actions. Checked on a computer only; the Ascuas style is as it was.
- **PC boxes, the trade's cards, the Union Room chat's keyboard and the letter in the Poke Ball look** (whole lower screen): the red ground down to the dark band, the screen's own pieces in the middle (a white card for the box's places and the hand, white and dark-red pills, a white pill for what is chosen, a two-line ring for the cursor) and the note under the band on the white: the box's message or the Pokémon under the cursor with its picture. The letter is a white card with its paper, its name and who wrote it, BACK on the band, and a touch of it closes it as before. The wireless lists and the waiting screen were already in this look. Checked on a computer only; the Ascuas style is as it was.
- **HOME in the Poke Ball look**: the buttons of HOME's layout (BACK, the plate and its turbo chip, the columns of menus, RUN, the X and Y keys) are flat pills with a dark-red edge instead of raised ones, the chosen or lit one white with a red mark, and the X and Y keys keep their red pill with the tab that was blue now white and its letter red. The name of the place in the note under the map sits in the middle of the white (and FLY, the zone's Pokémon and the Pokédex bar with it), and the RUN shoe has yellow where it was red so that it shows on the red pill. The same pills are used by every other screen drawn on HOME's layout in this style (the Union Room's lists, for instance). The map, its frame and the note under it are as they were; the Ascuas style is untouched. Checked on a computer only.
- **Battle move cards in the colour of their type** (Poke Ball style): each of the four cards is the colour of the move's type, with dark words on a light type and cream ones on a dark type, a white pill for the type, and a white edge on the one the cursor is on.

- **Lower screen HOME layout (test)**: on the plain field screen the map now sits in the middle of the lower screen, with POKéDEX, POKéMON, BAG and the trainer card in a column on its left and SAVE, OPTIONS and the X and Y items on its right. A band at the top holds the back key, a plate with your money and the turbo speed, a clock and the running switch; the note under the map keeps the place's name. Every other screen (bag, options, battle, PC...) keeps its layout. Checked on a computer only.
- **Pokemon of the zone on HOME's note** (test): the note under the map shows the Pokemon the zone's tables can give (grass, water, rocks, fishing; the night ones too), up to five, with a question mark while unseen and a small ball once caught; where all are met in water it says so.
- **The menus share HOME's layout** (test): SAVE, OPTIONS, the Bag (and the TM Case, the Berry Pouch, marts and the item PC), the Pokédex, the Trainer Card, the PC boxes, the move to forget and the move relearner, a letter and the field's choices now sit on the same floor as the map, with the top band (back key, plate, clock, running switch), the columns at the sides and a compact note under it. The status plate shows the page of OPTIONS or the place in the Bag's list where it fits. The party, the Summary, the naming keyboard, Easy Chat and the battle keep their full-screen look. Checked on a computer only.
- **Battle menu: the POKÉMON button** (test): its icon is now the party icon of the next Pokémon that can still fight (it bounces like the lead's), so it no longer repeats the Poké Ball of the button beside it. The Pokédex's habitat page (AREA) also sits in the new layout.
- **Status panel on HOME** (test): the plate at the top shows a Repel in force (its icon, the steps left and a bar) or else your money and the turbo speed, and a tap on it opens a panel over the map: the turbo (Off, x2, x3, x4), the three Repels with how many you have (a tap uses one, while none is in force) and your money. Where no wild Pokemon live, after the League the note says the next step of the Sevii postgame and, once the gems are restored, the national Pokedex count out of 386. The hints follow the game's own flags; their wording is a first draft.
- **Visible wilds** (PR #88 by mcl5500): aggressive wilds now run from your lead only when they have 15% of its power or less (before, below 70%), so they chase you far more; chasing land Pokémon may leave the tall grass for plain ground; new wilds appear near you but always just off the screen, and the map keeps alive a symmetric margin of 3 to 4 tiles of objects around the screen.
- **Naming screen, OPTIONS and battle prompts** (PR #88 by mcl5500): the naming keyboard is full width with 9 columns (the German one has the umlauts in the ninth), the OPTIONS page dots show one dot per page, the top screen's battle picture fades out with the rest at the end of a fight and holds the battle while the new Pokédex entry is shown, and the battle's Yes/No questions (the nickname of a caught Pokémon, learning a move, stopping, and the battle's own question box) are answered on the lower screen. Checked on a computer only.

### Removed

- Removed the optional Facets battle backgrounds from both Builders (the desktop app and command line `--battle-backgrounds`, and the web page's switch). The optional HD battle scenes are unchanged. A data pack made earlier with them keeps working.

### Fixed

- **Online play**: a sealed frame that the relay (or anyone on the way) sends a second time is no longer delivered twice, and a console whose line to the relay has gone silent for 45 seconds (Wi-Fi lost, a relay that hung) now closes the link by itself so the game can say so, instead of waiting for minutes.
- In the Poke Ball style the Summary's BACK button (and the arrows of the Bag's and the Pokédex's close buttons) were white on a white pill and could not be read; they take the pill's own ink now.

- HOME's status panel no longer stays open when a battle or a menu starts while it is up. After loading a game with a Repel in force, the plate and the bar read the right kind (before it always read the plain Repel, so a Super or Max Repel showed a full bar).
- The replay at the start of a game ("Previously on your quest", the Quest Log) tinted the map grey but left the player and the NPCs in colour when they use the Gen 4 pictures; their palettes follow the replay's tint now. The pictures of the Pokémon shown in a box (the starter in Oak's lab, for one) are brought from 80 to 64 pixels by the colour that covers most of each pixel, not by dropping every fifth row and column, so their lines are no longer broken.
- A Deoxys in the party or in the battle menu drew the other forme's icon (the Party passes the version's forme; the lower screen asked for the Normal one). Scrolling a list in the new menus no longer blinks: only the floor and the note are redrawn, and the keys and BACK are never shown greyed for a frame. The Pokédex and Bag notes fit their window.
- The Pokédex name, "Máx. Rep." and the Ruby hint on HOME's note and plate drew blank letters (their accents were encoded twice); the small font now also draws accented letters as the letter and a mark when the game's own cell is empty.
- Nuzlocke no longer releases the last Pokémon that can fight when only eggs would stay in the party.
- Eggs no longer gain experience under the EXP. Share options 1 and 2 (before, an egg in the party counted as a receiver, took its cut and could level up).
- The German naming keyboard has its ninth column again, so the full stop and the comma can be typed.
- The naming screen's question is copied up to its own end, so a long Spanish or German title no longer carries stack garbage after it.
- The HOME map's background worker thread is joined at exit before the data and assets shut down, so closing the game while the map is still being built cannot touch freed memory.
- **Packs with an empty file:** the Builder and the game could reject a pack when a payload had size 0 and shared its offset with the next one (the order of equal offsets depended on the file names). No pack made so far has an empty file, so nobody was affected yet.
- **Builder, desktop:** a pack that appeared at the destination while the Builder was working is no longer replaced when overwriting was not confirmed.
- **Web Builder:** a download that hangs ends after 60 seconds, a failing or killed Builder worker (usually for lack of memory on a phone) now shows a message instead of an endless progress bar, and choosing a second ROM while the first is still being checked can no longer leave the first one selected.
- **Easy Chat, A-Z screen:** the letters Y and Z had the same touch codes as the A-Z / Groups button and BACK, so tapping Y took you back to the groups and Z left the screen, and the selection ring of those buttons lit on the letters. They have codes of their own now.
- **Naming screen:** a space you typed was drawn as an empty box in the preview of the name.
- **Save file:** a new save file (a new game, or the copy of an old save the first time) is written beside its name and renamed into place, so a power cut in the middle no longer leaves a short file that the next start refuses for good. The copy of the save kept at each start now keeps the previous copy beside it (`twinembers.sav.bak.prev`), so a session that ended with a bad save does not cost the last good copy.
- **New game, choosing the name:** FireRed's own keyboard no longer shows on the upper screen. Oak's picture stays there while the lower screen's keyboard is used, for the player's and the rival's name. Checked only by reading the code and the host build; not yet run in the game.

## v0.6.0

### Added

- **Visible wild Pokémon (optional, off by default).** OPTIONS, Graphics, page Wilds: wild Pokémon walk in the grass and on the water of the map instead of appearing at random steps. WILD POKEMON sets how many (Off, Few 2, Normal 4, Many 6; an Old 3DS shows at most 2 at once, a New 3DS up to 6), WILD DISPLAY chooses the Pokémon or a silhouette, and AGGRESSIVE sets how many of them notice you and chase (Off, 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%); a much stronger lead makes them flee. The battle starts with the Pokémon you saw (species, level, gender, nature, shininess, or the roamer), and Repel works as it does for step encounters. The game's own step encounters stay where wilds cannot appear (Safari Zone, Randomlocke, no free room on the map). Ideas from PR #83 by mcl5500/Sh3ld0r and the design of Wilds of Hoenn by poooooby; credited, and none of their code is used.
- **Nuzlocke options, version 2.** One wild catch per area, fainted Pokémon released after the battle (announced in the game's message box), a new game clears the areas used, DUPLICATES and SHINY clauses, and LEVEL CAPS, on their own NUZLOCKE page of OPTIONS.
- **RANDOMLOCKE.** Wild and trainer Pokémon become random species, fixed by the save's Trainer ID; the starter in Oak's lab is random too.
- **REUSABLE TMS** (a TM is not used up when it teaches a move) and **FREE HMS** (an HM in the Bag works in the field with no Pokémon that knows it) in OPTIONS.
- **REMEMBER** in the party menu: FireRed's own move relearner, free and from anywhere, now drawn on the lower screen in every step (the move list, the Yes and No questions and the messages).
- **A world map on the lower screen's HOME page** built at run time from your own Region Map art: three zoom levels with + and - buttons, drag to pan, and a ribbon that cycles Kanto and the Sevii Islands. Fly works from the map shown, so Kanto and the Sevii destinations you have visited can be picked from either.
- **Native lower-screen screens for the mail and the Easy Chat**: the letter (with the stationery's name and who wrote it, and a tap on the letter closes it) and the phrase picker (phrase chips, groups, letters, the word list, Yes and No).
- **Pokédex page buttons** AREA and CRY on the entry's card, and an **EXP bar** on the Summary's STATS page.
- **RUN is greyed** when fleeing is impossible (trainer battles), and holding it keeps trying to flee.

### Improved

- **OPTIONS is organised in sections** (Settings, Graphics, Quality of life, Nuzlocke); the page button names the section. Switches have no arrows, each page has a colour edge and the page dots carry the page's name. Texts were shortened to fit their notes, and a test now measures that every label fits in English, Spanish and German.
- **Battle menu:** the lead's type badges in the FIGHT banner, a framed lead ball, crossed-out fainted balls, a colour for each condition, POKEMON showing how many are able, and the active Pokémon centred on its plate. The healthboxes lose the ball on the player's cap.
- **Bag:** the scroll rails are sliders; a tap jumps to where it was pressed and a drag follows the finger, in the field and the battle Bag.
- **Wording:** names of species, items, moves, types and maps, and the English and Spanish interface words, are in proper case like German; the player's name is shown as typed.
- **Shorter messages on the Save screen** that fit their note.
- **Builder and web Builder:** a failed download no longer hangs or reports success, Cancel works during a download and the free-space check is real. The old Ash and Green switch is gone from both Builders (the looks are picked in the game's OPTIONS).

### Fixed

- **Field people and Pokémon that share a picture** (for example the three Bird Keepers of Route 18, or two wilds of one species) were drawn with each other's frame: a walker slid without steps, or a trainer was drawn looking another way than it really looked. Each frame now has its own tiles. Checked on a computer only; it has not been run in the game yet.
- **A reversed Pokémon cry** (Growl, Hyper Voice) that played to its first sample crashed the console with a data abort (issue #87): the block index of the compressed sample was made with a logical shift, so sample -1 asked for an unmapped address. It now uses an arithmetic shift on the 3DS, which reads the same bytes the GBA read through its address mirror; playback of every other sample is unchanged. Checked with a model of the arithmetic and the assembled instruction; not yet run on a console.
- **Visible wilds, lost encounter:** a Pokémon that caught up with the player while they were between two tiles had its encounter thrown away (its wild then waited a second and a half and rested for ten seconds) instead of starting the battle when the step ended. The core now keeps the encounter until the player stands next to it, and drops it only for a reason (another height, a script or menu, it is no longer next to them, a battle already started); two Pokémon arriving together start one battle, and the other settles. Checked on a computer only.
- **Visible wilds, wandering:** a wandering Pokémon's second direction to try was the opposite of its first for south and west, and always the same turn for north and east. It is now a right angle chosen at random, and a wanderer no longer walks back over its last step. Checked on a computer only.
- **Battle Bag, L and R (issue #16):** the shoulder buttons walked the Bag's pockets (Items, Key Items, Poké Balls) under the title "Items", and the lower screen then walked back to Items, so the list jumped. The battle Bag is the Items pocket only (the balls have their own button, the Berry Pouch its BERRIES), so L and R now change no pocket there; the field Bag is unchanged. Checked on a computer only.
- **A battle animation** that moved a sprite through an address read with sign extension (issue #85) now builds the address from two 16-bit halves.
- **The evolution scene:** a B held since before the scene no longer stops the evolution, and the scene uses FireRed's plain background.
- **German text:** a double-encoded u-umlaut in the Summary's and the PC's German text.
- **Party action menu:** with more than 4 actions the menu is drawn in two columns and the D-pad now moves through them (up and down inside a column, left and right between columns). Checked on a computer only.
- **REMEMBER** no longer falls back to the GBA screen on its questions and messages. Checked on a computer only; it has not been run in the game yet.
- **The companion** no longer vanishes and reappears when you cross from one route to the next: what it remembers of the player's way is moved with the map's new coordinates. Warps and doors still hide it until your next step. Checked on a computer only; it has not been run in the game yet.
- **Labels that did not fit** their room on several lower-screen menus.
- **Menus:** the touch state of a closed screen no longer survives into the next one, and an empty Pokédex list is not scrolled.
- **Solar Beam and other moves with their own background:** over an HD battle picture the move's background is stretched to the whole screen above the text box, instead of leaving the painting in a strip above and beside it. Not yet seen in the game.
- **REMEMBER:** long messages no longer wait for A page by page while the lower screen already shows the whole text; the pages turn by themselves.
- **Sunny Day and other moves that light or darken the scene:** the HD battle picture now follows the blend of the scene's own colours, so the strip and the corner the move's picture does not cover are no longer left dark. Checked on a computer only (the colour maths); not yet seen in the game.
- **Battle scene:** the HD battle picture follows the screen's fades, so the end of a battle shows no black silhouettes.
- **Saving:** the save is committed with fsync, and a pack with a truncated back picture, or a follower asked for before the pack opens, no longer breaks the game.
- **Looks:** an HGSS look whose own walk sheet did not download is left out instead of being made from its sibling's.

### Performance

- **No stall at doors.** The day and night camera search was one frame of about 47 ms (Old 3DS) at each door; it now takes 16 frames of about 3 ms.
- **Cheaper lower screen:** fewer redrawn rectangles for the embers, less text work per frame, one Summary read a frame, and cached battle types. The FPS and fast-forward counters and the battle HUD draw runs of pixels, and the pack CRC32 uses a table.
- **The HOME map image** is built on a low-priority worker thread, so changing region no longer slows the game.

### Compatibility

- **German:** a 1-byte alignment fix in the German recipe (ViridianCity_Mart_Items and SayHiToOak), by mcl5500 (PR #84).
- **Settings file:** `settings.cfg` has a 512-byte block and still reads old 256-byte files. An old `visiblewilds=1` reads as Normal.
- **Packs:** build `twinembers.pak` again with the Builder of this release; it is made from your own ROM and no ROM or game data is distributed. The English, Spanish and German games are `twinembers.3dsx`, `twinembers-es.3dsx` and `twinembers-de.3dsx`.

### Interface

- **Sharper START-column icons** (Pokédex, Poké Ball, Bag, Trainer Card, Save, Options) redrawn pixel by pixel at 16x16 with colour ramps, an outline in each icon's own dark tone and a yellow hiker's backpack for the Bag, with a test that every word fits beside its icon in English, Spanish and German.
- **A new look for the lower screen** (the ember look, raised cream cards, flame accents and slate edges) on OPTIONS, HOME, the field and battle Bag, SAVE, the PC box and naming keyboard, the Pokédex, the Trainer Card, the Summary, the move-learn screens, the mail and the battle menus. The top screen's healthboxes and dialog panel match it.

### Known Issues

- **Visible wilds on an Old 3DS:** the frame time with 2 visible wilds has not been measured on a console (`port.log` `wilds:` lines are pending).
- **Visible wilds, edge cases not individually verified:** caves and Viridian Forest, the Pokémon Tower silhouettes, the Safari Zone, Randomlocke (step encounters should stay or come back), save and load with a wild on screen, and crowded maps (Celadon, Saffron) with 6 wilds. The owner played the visible wilds on real hardware and their general operation is correct; the list is in `docs/HARDWARE-ACCEPTANCE.md`, Overworld.
- **The 7 largest follower Pokémon** (Steelix, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Wailord, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza) are drawn at their HeartGold size and cover neighbouring tiles.
- **The shared-picture fix, the party menu D-pad and the native REMEMBER screens** have only been tested on a computer, not yet in the game or on a console.
- **Day and night and the companion** are implemented but still have no per-console result recorded; Old 3DS and 2DS models, long sessions and late-game content are not systematically tested (see `docs/VALIDATION.md`).
- **Real hardware coverage** is narrow: one New 3DS-family console and Azahar. Nothing else listed as unchecked in `docs/HARDWARE-ACCEPTANCE.md` should be taken as working.
- **The Safari Zone sign** still mentions START.
- **Repository CI:** the `source_audit.py --history` step currently fails on historical commits. It is not a game issue and does not change the release files.
